# E1 — Multi-event registry, qualification rules and coverage

**Checked:** 2026-10-11. Registry: [`configs/event_registry.json`](../configs/event_registry.json). Rules and validator: `src/www_events/registry.py`. Coverage matrix: [`reports/events/coverage_matrix.json`](../reports/events/coverage_matrix.json), regenerated by `uv run python -m www_events.coverage --checked-utc <UTC timestamp>`. Tests: `tests/test_events.py`.

This checkpoint turns the project from a March 2023 study into a multi-event design. March 2023 remains the anchor. It is also the only event measured so far, so **the three-independent-event target is not yet met**.

## Pre-specified rules

These were fixed before any event other than the anchor was measured. Changing them means changing `RULES` and recording why.

| Rule | Content |
|---|---|
| **R1 severity** | At least 30 traded minutes with \|log deviation from peg\| ≥ 100 bps inside some rolling 24-hour window. Only minutes with positive volume on the stablecoin leg count. A ≥ 500 bps extreme puts the event in the *severe* class. |
| **R2 independence** | One independent episode per primary trigger (cluster). Contagion legs, such as USDT in May 2022 or DAI and the USDT premium in March 2023, are `LINKED_LEG`s of their primary's cluster and never count as extra events. Candidates within 14 days that share a trigger are merged. |
| **R3 windows** | Baseline: 7 days, ending 2 days before the start, with a 1-day gap. Event: the reported dates. Recovery: the 7 days after the end. Matched control: 7 days ending 28 days before the start. ACCEPTED requires data covering the baseline, event and recovery windows; control coverage is tracked separately. |
| **R4 sourcing** | SOURCE_DOCUMENTED requires sources from ≥ 2 distinct publishers. Reported prices are kept as `reported_extreme` and are never treated as measurements. |
| **Out-of-sample** | Events starting on or after 2024-01-01 are pre-registered as held out. They are used only after the in-sample design (2020–2023) is frozen. |
| **Negative cases** | Candidates expected to fail R1 (June 2023 USDT) and shocks without a reported depeg (BUSD issuance halt, a `PLACEBO`) stay in the registry and are measured under the same rules. |

Statuses, in order of evidence: `CANDIDATE` → `SOURCE_DOCUMENTED` → `DATA_VERIFIED` (R1 measured on acquired, hashed data) → `ACCEPTED` (R1 passes and windows are covered). The other statuses are `REJECTED` and `UNRESOLVED`, and both require a reason.

## Measured evidence (anchor only)

`reports/events/E2023-03-USDC-SVB_r1.json`, measured on the audited IAQF panel (SHA-256 `17704ff0…11b3`):
- Binance.US USDC/USD passes R1. The worst 24-hour window has 1,379 breaching traded minutes. The extreme is −1,470 bps (severe class). Breaches run from 2023-03-11 02:08 to 2023-03-13 18:50 UTC.
- Applied to the same panel, R1 also flags a USDT/USD *premium* of up to +164 bps on 2023-03-12 (387 breaching minutes, 14:47–22:44 UTC). This shows why R2 matters: the rule finds the flight to USDT, which is a leg of the same crisis, not a new event.

## What this means for the paper

- **Independent episodes currently measurable from free, listed public data:** UST (2022, in-sample), the USDC anchor (2023), and TUSD, FDUSD and USDe (out-of-sample). The BUSD placebo is also listed. Together these would give three or more independent clusters spanning four mechanism types, *if* R1 is confirmed on downloaded data.
- **Quote-basis caveat:** the Binance.com files are quoted in USDT, so they measure deviation against USDT, not USD. That is acceptable only in windows where USDT/USD is itself verified near par. It is a problem for the May 2022 and November 2022 clusters, where USDT was stressed, and needs a USD reference venue.
- **No executable depth.** The listed files are 1-minute klines. They support descriptive price analysis only, not capacity (see the C2 gate).

## Decisions needed

1. **Authorize downloading free public data files** from `data.binance.vision`: monthly 1-minute klines for USTUSDT, USTBUSD, TUSDUSDT, FDUSDUSDT, USDEUSDT, BUSDUSDT and USDCUSDT, and BTCUSDT for cross-rates. That is about 15 files of roughly 0.5–4 MB each, judging from listed sizes such as 931,777 bytes for USDEUSDT 2025-10. Binance's public data terms would need a quick review first. Without this, no further event can move past SOURCE_DOCUMENTED.
2. **USD reference venue for USDT-stress clusters.** Optional sources are Binance.US public history and Coinbase/Kraken public candles. Each also needs download permission.

## Event protocol runner

`src/www_events/runner.py` applies the same protocol to any registry event. It builds the R3 windows, then loads local data: the IAQF panel, or Binance archives that must each pass their `.CHECKSUM`. It converts USDT-quoted prices to USD only when a USDT/USD leg is supplied, and otherwise labels the result `USDT_RELATIVE` with a warning. Then it applies R1 over the event window ±1 day and reports diagnostics for each window. Command for the anchor:

```sh
uv run python -m www_events.runner --event-id E2023-03-USDC-SVB \
  --iaqf-panel /path/to/panel_1min.parquet --stable bnus_usdcusd --quote-basis USD \
  --output reports/events/E2023-03-USDC-SVB_protocol.json
```

A Binance event would run with `--binance-dir data/raw/binance --stable USDEUSDT --quote-basis USDT` once the files are authorized and acquired.

**Anchor result** (`reports/events/E2023-03-USDC-SVB_protocol.json`; Binance.US USDC/USD, traded minutes):

| Window | Dates | Traded minutes | Median abs dev | P95 abs dev | Share ≥ 100 bps | Half-life to par, 15-min bins |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| baseline | 03-02 → 03-08 | 1,946 | 1.0 bps | 3.0 | 0% | 22.6 min |
| event | 03-10 → 03-13 | 4,563 | 209.2 bps | 950.8 | 60% | 1,211.7 min |
| recovery | 03-14 → 03-20 | 2,889 | 5.0 bps | 26.6 | 0% | 82.3 min |
| control | 02-04 → 02-10 | not covered | — | — | — | — |

**Interpretation, used to design the cross-event analysis.** The *peg dislocation* (USDC's own USD price) persisted for about a day per half-life during the event. Over the same window, the *within-venue triangle wedge* (C3) reverted within minutes at minute resolution and within about 13–16 minutes on 15-minute bins. These are two separate objects. Cross-quote arbitrage kept relative prices aligned while the USD value of USDC itself stayed dislocated, which points to redemption and settlement frictions rather than cross-quote execution. This is a descriptive contrast for one event. Treat the half-life as a description of the window, not a structural estimate: the level moved away from par and back within the window. Whether the same separation holds across events is the first cross-event question for E2.

## Pre-analysis-plan quantities (anchor, EXPLORATORY)

The runner now emits a `plan` block for each event (`src/www_events/plan_dvs.py`), implementing plan sections 2, 5 and 6 and amendment A1. Anchor values below come from `reports/events/E2023-03-USDC-SVB_protocol.json`. The anchor was measured before the freeze, so they are labelled `EXPLORATORY` and excluded from every confirmatory count.

- **DV1.** The control window (February 2023) has 0% coverage, so the reference falls back to the baseline, and the result is flagged. Median \|D\| in the event window minus the baseline is **+208 bps** (95% CI 92–336, block-bootstrap SE 74). The p-value sits at its floor of 1/1,000 draws.
- **DV3.** The extreme was −1,470 bps at 2023-03-11 07:55 UTC. The trailing median \|D\| (from the extreme onward) first fell below 25 bps at 2023-03-14 04:19 UTC, giving a recovery time of **4,104 minutes (≈ 68 h)**, uncensored.
- **Covariates (associations only).**
  - BTC/USD realized volatility: 40 bps/h in the baseline, 80 in the event window, 85 in recovery. Market-wide volatility roughly doubled, which is an alternative explanation to report next to any capacity interpretation.
  - Traded-minute share on the USDC/USD leg: 19% in the baseline, 79% in the event window, 29% in recovery.
  - The IAQF panel carries closes through no-trade minutes, so its "missing minutes" are always 0. Activity shows up through the traded-minute share instead.
