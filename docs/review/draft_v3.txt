375      What price feeds should do. A feed should label the unit of each                  433
376   exchange price and correct with the exchange’s rate rather than                      434
377   drop the exchange. The rate is cheap to estimate: it needs a handful                 435
378   of unrelated assets on each exchange and one reference exchange.                     436
379   The rate also names which exchanges share a dollar, which a plain                    437
380   median or source weighting does not (Appendix A).                                    438
381                                                                                        439
382      Limits. The diagnostic rests on three USD exchanges, and Bit-                     440
383   stamp lists only bitcoin and ether, so the zero correction on the con-               441
384   trol pair partly reflects the estimator’s rule of applying none when                 442
385   fewer than two assets are visible. When two of three exchanges                       443
386   share a biased dollar, prices alone cannot say which unit is the U.S.                444
387   dollar; an outside anchor, such as a working bank rail, is needed.                   445
