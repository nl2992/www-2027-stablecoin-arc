## Notes and coverage gaps

- **Bitfinex 2017-2019:** well documented but the first Wells Fargo report is uncertain by a few days, and 2018 delays rely on retrospective sources. Bitfinex's own blog/status posts were not retrieved.
- **Bitstamp:** only the March 2023 Silvergate step and a doubtful 2020 CNG dispute were found. 2017-2022 returned nothing, but searching was shallow (no status-page or blog archive). Treat as 'not searched well', not 'no event'. Its Signet USD rail after 2023-03-12 is undocumented.
- **Coinbase / GDAX / Pro:** no USD deposit/withdrawal impairment found for 2017-2021 (searched; mostly spam results). Late-2017 volume backlogs are widely remembered but I found no dated source; this is likely 'not searched well'. Status-page history was not accessible.
- **Kraken:** 2017-2019 and 2021-2022 returned nothing. The Fidor USD pause (Feb 2014) is out of range. Kraken's own ACH restoration date after March 2023 was not found.
- **Binance.US:** June 2023 and Feb 2025 are well sourced. The date USD withdrawals fully ended (late June vs 2023-07-13) is uncertain. Nothing found for Oct 2022-Feb 2023 and Mar 2025 onward; the Silvergate/Signature closures had no reported Binance.US service impact until June.
- **Confounders** are a sample of well-documented events, not a complete census. Exchange outages (e.g. Coinbase May 2021 volume outages) were not verified and are omitted. Hack/outage lists for Bitstamp 2017-2023 were not found.
- Several entries rest on search-result summaries because the page returned 403 or timed out (flagged in `notes`). Evidence quality refers to the first report as sourced, mostly secondary relays of exchange statements; few primary exchange pages were retrieved.
- Signet was never formally shut down: it ran in FDIC receivership and Coinbase chose to pause it. Label it as partner-driven, not a rail shutdown by the bank.
